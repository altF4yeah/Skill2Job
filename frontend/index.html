Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Skill2Job.

How does Skill2Job work?

Skill2Job analyzes your uploaded resume using AI, extracts your skills, compares them with your selected career role, and generates personalized career insights along with a learning roadmap.

What file formats can I upload?

You can upload your resume in PDF or DOCX format. The AI reads your resume and identifies both technical and soft skills.

Can I choose my preferred job role?

Yes. Before analysis, you can select your target role such as Software Engineer, Data Analyst, AI Engineer, Web Developer, and more.
